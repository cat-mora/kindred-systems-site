What is lead conversion?

Lead conversion is the process of turning an enquiry into a paying client. For a high-value service business, that process may include the first response, qualification, consultation booking, attendance, discovery, proposal, finance discussion, follow-up and final decision.

Improving lead conversion means finding where suitable prospects leave that process and changing the timing, message, responsibility or system involved.
