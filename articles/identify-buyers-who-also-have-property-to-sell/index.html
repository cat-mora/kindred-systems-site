How can real estate agents identify buyers who also have a property to sell?

Lead conversion in specific industries 4 min read · Published 20 September 2026 · Updated 20 September 2026

Ask buyers whether they need to sell another property before they can buy, then record the answer and who will follow it up. Listing portals may already tell you that a buyer has a home to sell, but the information is easily missed. Answer their buying question first, then offer help with timing or an appraisal without turning the conversation into a sales pitch.

A buyer and a future seller are often the same person. The information may appear in a portal checkbox, an open-home conversation, a finance discussion or a comment about timing. If it stays buried in notes, nobody will remember to follow up. Start by understanding the person's move, rather than trying to force an appraisal appointment into the first conversation.

Ask about the sale early

Audit the fields received from each listing portal and form. Some data may be visible in the notification but not mapped into the CRM. Check whether agents ask a consistent question and whether yes, later and unsure produce different tasks. Review privacy notices and permissions so the agency uses the information for an expected and lawful purpose.

  • A portal notification says the buyer has a property to sell, but that information is missing from the CRM.

  • Agents keep the information in personal notes.

  • Every buyer is asked for an appraisal before their buying question is answered.

  • Nobody follows up potential sellers when the listing agent is busy.

  • The agency cannot see how many active buyers also have a property to sell.

Record it and follow up

Ask a natural question about the buyer's timing and whether their purchase depends on a sale. If it does, ask whether they would like help understanding the likely value, preparation or order of events. Record only the information you need, including the area, expected timing and whether they want another agent to contact them. Explain any introduction so it feels like coordinated service, not like their details were passed around.

1. Check that listing portals and forms pass sale-related answers into the CRM.

2. Agree on one respectful question agents can ask during buyer conversations.

3. Record whether a sale is involved, the likely timing, permission to follow up and the staff member responsible.

4. Suggest a market discussion or later appraisal when it suits the buyer's timing.

5. Review these enquiries regularly so they are not forgotten after the open home.

What to track

Track the number of buyers who disclose a sale requirement, the proportion offered relevant help, appointments accepted and eventual listings, while also monitoring complaints and duplicate contact. Do not use a vendor conversion target to distort buyer service. Validate fields and permissions when portals, CRMs or agency processes change.

What to avoid

  • Assuming a portal checkbox is always current or complete.

  • Contacting the person about selling before answering their buying question.

  • Passing their details to another agent without explaining who will contact them and why.

  • Using the same appraisal campaign regardless of timing.

  • Failing to update the record after the person has sold or changed plans.

Common questions

When should the agent ask?

After addressing the immediate property question, ask how the purchase fits with their current home and timing. Keep it conversational and optional.

What if they are not ready for an appraisal?

Record the timing they give, offer useful general information and agree whether a later check-in would help.

Can AI identify these people from notes?

It may assist under privacy and accuracy controls, but the agency should verify the result and avoid inferring sensitive or unsupported details.

A realistic example

Example: A portal enquiry includes a ticked box saying the buyer has a property to sell. The agent answers the listing question first, then confirms that the buyer expects to sell in three months and would like a market discussion closer to that date. The agent explains who will call and records the agreed date. This is an example.
