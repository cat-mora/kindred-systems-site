How Kindred approaches AI

How this gets done, in practice.

Every engagement starts with what you already believe you need AI to do, and that’s usually a good starting brief. From there, the work is finding whatever inside that brief will do the most for your cost, your revenue, or the value of the business itself, using the tools you already have rather than a new piece of software to buy.

Sometimes that’s exactly what you expected. Sometimes it becomes clear once the work is underway that the bigger opportunity sits somewhere slightly different, and that gets worked through together, not sprung on you afterwards. None of this is improvised. I work to a proven framework built to get you a result, not to keep you relying on me longer than you need to.

What you’re left with isn’t a slide deck, and it’s often not something new either. More often it’s a tool or workflow you already had, finally used to its full potential, because I’ve gone looking for whatever’s most likely to move the needle fastest: something that’s worked before, or something nobody has tried yet. Either way, it becomes a real asset: your own people can run it and keep building on it for years after the engagement ends. The point isn’t to keep you relying on me. It’s for the result to keep growing once I’m gone.
