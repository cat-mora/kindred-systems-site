What does an Insights article on this site look like?

By Cathryn Mora · Published 13 September 2026 · Last updated 13 September 2026

Every Insights article on this site follows the same structure: a direct answer to one specific question in the opening lines, then short sections under clear subheadings that add detail, examples or context. This page is a working example of that structure, not a live piece of advice.

Why the answer comes first

Someone reading this page, or an AI assistant summarising it, should get the answer in the first few lines, not after several paragraphs of setup. That is the point of an Insights article: answer one specific question well, then support the answer underneath.

What stays the same on every article

  • A clear, specific H1 that states the question the article answers.
  • A direct-answer paragraph immediately under the H1, before any other content.
  • Short H2 sections, one idea per section, in place of long unbroken paragraphs.
  • A visible byline crediting Cathryn Mora, linked to /about/.
  • A visible "published" and "last updated" line.
  • A visible breadcrumb (Home, Insights, the article), the same as the BreadcrumbList in the page's structured data.
  • Article schema in the page's JSON-LD: headline, datePublished, dateModified, and author and publisher references pointing at the existing Person and Organization records already defined on /about/ and the homepage.

What this page is not

This page is not a finished article, and it is marked noindex so it will not appear in search results. Real Insights articles will be built from this same structure once their topics are confirmed.

How to use this template

Copy this page's HTML file, update the title, meta description, canonical link, JSON-LD URLs and dates, remove the noindex tag, replace this body content with the real article, then add the new URL to sitemap.xml and to the article grid on /insights/. Full step-by-step notes are left as a comment in this page's HTML source.
