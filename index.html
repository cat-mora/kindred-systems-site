What this looks like inside the business.

  • Good enquiries don’t answer the first call or email, and nobody tries again.
  • Leads that aren’t ready today get forgotten instead of nurtured for when they are.
  • Nobody can say how many of last month’s enquiries became paying clients, or why the rest didn’t.
  • Older leads and past enquirers sit untouched in a spreadsheet or CRM.
  • Follow-up happens when someone has time, not on a process everyone follows.
  • The same handful of enquiries get the attention, while the rest wait their turn and go cold.
Why this keeps happening

It’s rarely about how many enquiries you get.

  • Follow-up depends on whoever has time that day, not a defined process.
  • Nobody owns what happens after someone enquires, so it comes down to who remembers.
  • Older leads get written off instead of re-engaged.
  • Without a clear view of where enquiries drop off, fixing it is guesswork.
  • The team’s stretched, so the fastest response wins, and it isn’t always yours.
What changes

Picture six months from now.

Revenue is up, because more of the enquiries you’re already generating are becoming clients, and you can say exactly why. The board stops asking where the growth is coming from, because you already told them. The enquiries you’re already paying to generate are turning into clients instead of stalling. A decision that used to sit in someone’s inbox for three weeks now gets made in three days, because somebody owns it and there’s a number attached to it. Staff who spent hours a week chasing up leads by hand, copying information between systems and updating spreadsheets, are doing less of that and more of the work that needs their judgement.

  • More money in: sales that used to stall now close
  • Less money out: the tool you’re already paying for finally earns its keep
  • Hours back: staff freed from repetitive admin
  • One clear owner, so decisions stop waiting for someone to sign off
  • A straight answer, ready before the board asks for one

In a nutshell: save money, make more money, or both.

How this gets done

How this gets done, in practice.

Every engagement starts with the one thing you most want to change: more clients from the enquiries you already get, work off your plate, or a straight answer on where growth is coming from. That’s usually a good starting brief. From there, the work is finding whatever inside that brief will do the most for your cost, your revenue, or the value of the business itself, using the tools you already have rather than a new piece of software to buy. Sometimes that’s exactly what you expected. Sometimes it becomes clear once the work is underway that the bigger opportunity lies somewhere slightly different, and that gets worked through together, not sprung on you afterwards. Either way, what’s left behind is practical: a process, a short set of rules, and training your own people can run, so your team keeps it going instead of paying an adviser to stay in the room.

Frequently asked questions.

A few questions that tend to come up before someone books a Discovery Call.

What is Kindred Systems?

Kindred Systems is an independent advisory practice, founded by Cathryn Mora, that helps high-value Australian service businesses turn more of their enquiries into clients, using automation tools, including AI, where relevant.

How does an engagement work, in practice?

Every engagement starts with the one thing you most want to change, usually more clients from the enquiries you already get. From there, the work is finding whatever inside that brief will do the most for your cost, your revenue or the value of the business, using the tools you already have rather than a new piece of software to buy. What’s left behind is practical: a process, a short set of rules and training your own people can run, so your team keeps it going once the engagement ends.

Who is Kindred Systems built for?

Established, founder-led Australian service businesses, generally with at least A$500,000 in annual revenue, that want more of the enquiries they’re already generating to become clients. Larger organisations at the executive level can work with Kindred Systems through the Executive AI Sprint instead.

Do we need to already be using AI or a CRM to work with you?

No. Kindred Systems works with businesses at any stage: some are already using AI or automation and want more from it, others aren’t using either yet. The starting point is always what happens between an enquiry coming in and it becoming a client, not the tools already in place.

Does the work stay with our team after the engagement ends?

Yes. Whatever gets built together, whether that’s a workflow, a process or a set of rules, stays with your people. The aim is for your team to keep running it and building on it long after the engagement is over, not to keep paying an adviser to stay in the room.
