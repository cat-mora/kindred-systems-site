Why this is happening

AI already recommends businesses like yours.

People ask AI tools to recommend businesses every day. "What's the best accountant in Brisbane?" "Which project management software is worth paying for?" "Who does commercial cleaning in Melbourne CBD?" AI answers those questions, and it names specific businesses when it does.

These tools don't browse a website the way a person does. They crawl it with AI agents that strip out images, often ignore JavaScript, and look for specific signals to decide whether the content is trustworthy enough to cite. Most websites were built for human eyes, so they fail those checks without anyone noticing.

This isn't a future problem. Most competitors haven't fixed it yet either, which leaves a window to get ahead of it now.

What we look at

What the scorecard checks.

These are the seven factors behind your AI visibility score. They overlap with SEO in places, but they aren't the same checklist.

AI crawler access

Whether robots.txt is blocking AI crawlers such as GPTBot, ClaudeBot and PerplexityBot. If AI can't crawl your site, it can't recommend you.

llms.txt and AI-readable signals

A newer file format that tells AI agents what your business does and which content on your site counts most. Most sites still don't have one.

Structured data (schema)

Markup such as FAQ and Organization schema helps AI understand what questions your content answers. Sites with good schema are more likely to be cited directly.

Content structure

Whether headings, paragraphs and answers are written so an AI agent can extract and quote them. Direct answers to real questions score well.

Server-side rendering

Whether your core content shows up in the raw HTML, or depends on a browser to build the page. AI agents often see raw HTML only.

E-E-A-T signals

Author credentials, citations and real-world expertise signals. AI tools are trained to prefer sources that demonstrate genuine authority.

Site speed and crawl health

Slow pages and broken internal links create problems for any crawler, including AI. We check for the basics that block access.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions.

What is AEO?

AEO stands for answer engine optimisation, sometimes called AI search optimisation. It means structuring a website and a business's wider online presence so tools such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Google's AI Overviews can find, understand and recommend the business, in much the same way SEO structures a site for traditional search rankings.

How do I get my business recommended by ChatGPT?

Start with the basics: confirm ChatGPT's crawlers can reach your site, write your key facts (what you do, where you operate, who you serve) in plain, direct language rather than burying them in design elements, and add structured data such as FAQ schema. The free AI Visibility Scorecard checks all of this and shows exactly where your site currently stands.

How do I get found in AI search?

The same fundamentals apply across AI search tools: crawlable pages, clear direct-answer content, structured data, and credible, checkable expertise signals. Run the free scorecard to see which of these your site already has and which ones need work.

How can I improve my visibility in ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini?

Each tool crawls and reads slightly differently, but the underlying signals overlap significantly: technical access, content structure, schema markup and E-E-A-T (experience, expertise, authority and trust) signals affect all three. Fixing those signals lifts visibility across the board rather than one tool at a time.

Does AEO also help Google SEO?

Yes. There's a large overlap: crawlability, structured data, content quality and topical authority count toward both traditional Google rankings and AI answer engines.

What is AI search optimisation?

AI search optimisation is another name for AEO: the practice of making a website and business easy for AI systems to find, read and recommend, alongside the work you're already doing for traditional search engines.

How long does AEO take to work?

Technical fixes, such as crawler access or schema markup, can be live within days to weeks. Building the authority and citation signals that get a business recommended more consistently takes longer, generally two to four months.
