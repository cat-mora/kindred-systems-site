AI Search Visibility

Can ChatGPT find your business? Here's what that means.

Last updated: 21 October 2026

AI Search Visibility, also called AEO or answer engine optimisation, is whether tools such as ChatGPT, Google's AI Overviews, Gemini and Perplexity can find your business, understand what it does, and recommend it when someone asks a buying question in your industry. It works alongside traditional SEO rather than replacing it, and most businesses have never checked where they stand.

People already ask AI tools who to call for a service like yours, and AI already answers by naming specific businesses. If yours isn't one of the names it gives, you're invisible at the exact moment someone is ready to buy.

Why this is happening

AI already recommends businesses like yours

People ask AI tools to recommend businesses every day. "Who's the best family lawyer in Brisbane?" "Which pool builder should I use on the Gold Coast?" "Who does cosmetic dentistry near me?" AI answers those questions, and it names specific businesses when it does.

These tools don't browse a website the way a person does. They crawl it with AI agents that often strip out images, skip JavaScript, and look for specific signals before deciding a page is trustworthy enough to cite. Most websites were built for a person reading on a screen, so they fail those checks without anyone noticing.

This isn't a future problem, and most competitors haven't fixed it yet either.

What we look at

What AI Search Visibility checks

These are the main factors behind whether AI tools can find, read and recommend a business. They overlap with SEO in places, but they aren't the same checklist.

AI crawler access

Whether your robots.txt file is blocking AI crawlers such as GPTBot, ClaudeBot and PerplexityBot. If AI can't crawl your site, it can't recommend you.

llms.txt and AI-readable signals

A newer file format that tells AI agents what your business does and which content on your site counts most. Most sites still don't have one.

Structured data (schema)

Markup such as FAQ and Organization schema helps AI understand what questions your content answers. Sites with clear schema are more likely to be cited directly.

Content structure

Whether headings, paragraphs and answers are written so an AI agent can pull out a direct quote. Content that answers a real question plainly tends to perform well.

Server-side rendering

Whether your core content shows up in the raw HTML, or depends on a browser to build the page first. AI agents often only ever see the raw HTML.

E-E-A-T signals

Author credentials, citations and real-world expertise signals. AI tools are built to prefer sources that can show genuine authority.

Site speed and crawl health

Slow pages and broken internal links create problems for any crawler, AI included. We check for the basics that block access before anything else.

Common questions

Questions about AI Search Visibility

What is AEO?

AEO stands for answer engine optimisation, also called AI Search Visibility or AI search optimisation. It means structuring a website and a business's wider online presence so tools such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Google's AI Overviews can find, understand and recommend the business, in a similar way to how SEO structures a site for traditional search rankings.

How is this different from normal SEO?

There's a real overlap: crawlability, structured data and content quality count toward both. The difference is in what each system rewards. Google's traditional search ranks pages. An AI tool reads a page, decides whether it trusts it enough to use, then writes its own answer in its own words, often without sending the person to your site at all. Optimising for that means writing in a way AI can quote directly and adding the structured data it looks for, on top of the SEO work you're already doing.

How do I know if my business currently shows up in ChatGPT?

Ask it the kind of question a prospective customer would ask, naming your industry and location rather than your business by name, and see whether you're one of the businesses it mentions. That gives you a rough read. A deeper check goes further: it looks at why you do or don't appear, and what's blocking or helping you.

Can you guarantee my business gets recommended by ChatGPT?

No. AI Search Visibility can be measured, tracked and improved, the same discipline that has always applied to SEO, but no one can honestly guarantee a specific recommendation or position inside ChatGPT or any other AI tool. What's deliverable is a clear, measured improvement in how findable and well represented your business is.

What's the difference between the report, the audit and the sprint?

The report sets out the full findings: what's working, what isn't, and a prioritised list of what to fix first. The audit goes deeper, with Cathryn involved directly across your site, schema, content and citations. The foundation sprint is where the highest-priority fixes get built over a focused block of weeks, rather than left as a list for your own team to action. Which one makes sense depends on your current site and your team's capacity, and that's exactly what the Discovery Call is for.

Is this just a technical fix, or does my website's content count too?

Both. Technical access, whether AI can crawl your site at all, is the starting point, but an AI tool still has to read your content and decide it answers the question well enough to use. Pages that state plainly what you do, where you operate and who you serve tend to perform far better than pages written mainly to look good on screen.

How long does it take to see a difference?

Technical fixes, such as crawler access or schema markup, can be live within days to weeks. Building the credibility and citation signals that get a business recommended more consistently takes longer, generally a few months. Visibility can be measured from the first check, so you can see the change happening rather than guessing at it.

Do I need a new website to do this?

Usually not. Most of the work happens within your existing site: fixing crawler access, adding structured data, and adjusting how key pages are written. A full rebuild is only needed in the rare case where the platform itself is blocking AI crawlers altogether.

Does this replace Lead Conversion Advisory, or compete with it?

Neither. AI Search Visibility is about being found and recommended in the first place. Lead Conversion Advisory looks at what happens after someone enquires, from first response through to becoming a client. Plenty of businesses only need one of the two. Where someone needs both, they work well together: visibility brings the enquiry in, and a stronger conversion process makes sure it doesn't go to waste.

Can't I just ask ChatGPT to recommend my business myself?

You can ask it today and see what it says, and it's worth doing. What you can't do from inside a chat window is see why it answered that way, or know for certain what to change. That needs a full look at your site's crawl access, structured data and content, checked against what affects whether AI tools trust and cite a business.
